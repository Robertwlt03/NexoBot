# Wood texture

`walnut.webp`: walnut slab by [UNIBOA](https://unsplash.com/@uniboa) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/WPPT8uFH-cY),
used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Cropped to the lower band of the slab (no crack under the engraving) and stored as WebP.

`fir.webp` (light mode): Douglas fir board by [Christopher Stark](https://unsplash.com/@christopherstark) on
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/2SNC9jVruBc), under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
A full-resolution band (3954 × 1220) cut upright out of the photo, so the grain is not stretched, in the wood's own
colours, stored as WebP.
