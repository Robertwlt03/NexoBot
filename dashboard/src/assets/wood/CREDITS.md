# Wood texture

`walnut.webp`: walnut slab by [UNIBOA](https://unsplash.com/@uniboa) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/WPPT8uFH-cY),
used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Cropped to the lower band of the slab (no crack under the engraving) and stored as WebP.

`oak.webp` (light mode): golden oak board by [Manuel Gast](https://unsplash.com/@gama26) on
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/xC7j47IbMBQ), under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
A band cut upright out of the middle of the photo (no rotation, no stretching), scaled to 2880 × 889 in the wood's own
colours, stored as WebP.
